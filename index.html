Our story

A home atelier, one batch at a time

Dori Yums began in a home kitchen in Algiers, with a piping bag and a simple idea: desserts taste better when they are made for someone. Every shell is piped by hand, every filling is folded in small batches, and nothing goes into the oven until you order it.

Whether you need a dozen macarons for tea or a full dessert table for a wedding, each order is planned with you — colors, flavors and finishing touches matched to your moment.

How to order

Choose your flavors

Pick from the twelve signature flavors, or describe a theme — colors, an occasion, a favorite taste. Screenshots and inspiration photos are welcome.

Send it on WhatsApp

Message us your selection, quantity and date. We reply personally — this is a home atelier, not a call center.

Start the conversation

We confirm together

Price, payment and timing are agreed in the chat before any baking starts — no surprises, no fine print.

Pickup or delivery

Collect your order in Algiers or have it delivered to your door, fresh from the atelier and boxed for the occasion.

Custom and event orders are baked to schedule — message us a few days ahead of your date.
